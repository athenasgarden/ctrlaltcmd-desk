# Ship rules

Quality beats a nightly quota. A thin page published to hit a target is a liability on the root domain.

## Ship

A page a stranger can use without the rest of the catalog. Exact destination URLs. Honest prices and limits. A date on anything that will rot.

## Do not ship

Paid files given away on GitHub. Coming-soon homepages. Interview or revenue guarantees. Cold X pitches. Email that is not SEND APPROVED. Ads without ADS APPROVED and a cap.

## Recurring machines

- Homeschool IRL Pins: queue, not spray.
- Athena ATS SEO: one slug when the last one is live. No invented conversion numbers.
- Mogul city page: only when it is actually useful.
- This desk: update DESK + CATALOG when a URL becomes real. Do not add vapor.

## Voice

Direct. Operator. No dream-job copy on ATS pages. Spiritual work and fiction stay in their own lanes.

## Going forward

New public URL: add a CATALOG row, a DESK table line, and an index.html link. Leave AFTERNOON.md alone unless the runbook broke.
