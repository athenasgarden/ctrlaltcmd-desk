# Tailor one resume to one job in 45 minutes

No account. No new template. One posting you can actually do.

## Before the timer (5 minutes)

Open three things only: the full job description, your resume as editable text, a blank note titled company + role.

Write the must-haves the posting repeats or puts under required. Cap at eight. If you cannot clear three of them on paper, stop and apply somewhere else.

## Pass 1 — steal the language that is already true (15 minutes)

Highlight tools, methods, and scope phrases that appear more than once in the JD.

For each highlight, find one bullet you already have that is the same work under different words. Change your wording to their wording only where it is true.

Illegal: adding a tool you watched a video about. You will be asked.

Put the three strongest matches in the top third of Experience.

## Pass 2 — the file the portal can read (10 minutes)

Single column. Headings in plain text: Experience, Education, Skills. .docx or a text-based PDF. Contact in the body. Dates next to each role.

After you upload, open the parsed fields and fix titles that landed in Skills.

## Pass 3 — Skills and summary (10 minutes)

Skills: only tools from the JD you can defend on a call. Summary: two lines. Role you want + two facts from this JD that you have lived.

## Send (5 minutes)

Upload. Fix autofill. Submit. Log company, date, tailored Y/N. Five of these in a week beats forty Easy Apply taps.
