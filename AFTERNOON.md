# A three-hour afternoon (yours)

Use this on your job search, product, or homeschool day.

## 20 minutes — brief
Four lines: what you already have, who it is for this month, what done looks like, what you will not open. If you cannot name who it is for, do not start a new project.

## 100 minutes — one surface
Apply (one JD, one tailored resume), sell (one page or email about a thing that works), or teach (one day from the free homeschool week). Stop when the time ends.

## 40 minutes — send or save
Send the application or put the draft where tomorrow-you will see it.

## 20 minutes — close
What left the building. What is waiting. What tomorrow inherits (one item). Then stop.
