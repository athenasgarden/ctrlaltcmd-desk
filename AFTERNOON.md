# Afternoon runbook (3 hours)

Clock starts when the tab opens. One object on the desk.

## 0:00–0:20 — pick the job

Write four lines:

1. What already exists (URL or file path)
2. Who would pay or use it this month
3. The smallest proof that counts as done today
4. What you will not touch

If line 2 is blank, you do not get a new feature. You get a sales artifact or Aether.  
If line 2 is job seekers with a JD open, the afternoon belongs to Athena ATS — one surface, not three.

## 0:20–2:00 — one surface

One repo or one store. Public text must be usable without the rest of the brand. Destination URLs are real. No ads without ADS APPROVED plus a dollar cap.

Good: a guide someone can run tomorrow, a live-product sitemap patch, five unsent drafts, a sign-in bug.  
Bad: a new subdomain with two sentences, renaming the company, a third agent wrapper.

## 2:00–2:40 — ship or queue

Ship means public host or a dated folder with status `awaiting_approval` | `live` | `dead`. If a human still has to click Deploy / Pages / SEND APPROVED, that is the last line.

## 2:40–3:00 — close

Three sentences in notes/YYYY-MM-DD.md: what shipped, what is blocked on a human, what tomorrow inherits. Then stop.

If the afternoon explodes: one file, one URL, one sentence of proof. That still counts.
