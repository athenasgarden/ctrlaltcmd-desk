# Get paid for something you already shipped

You do not need another feature. You need one person to give you money for what exists. Ninety minutes. One object.

## 1. Name the object (10 minutes)

"I already made ____. A person with ____ problem can use it this week."

If you cannot fill both blanks, you do not have a product yet. Finish the thing or pick a different thing that already works.

Counts: a live URL, a PDF they can download, a session you can run Thursday, a script they can paste.
Does not count: a pitch deck, a platform, a waitlist with no file behind it.

## 2. Price without a funnel (10 minutes)

One number you would actually accept this month. One-time is easier than a subscription you cannot support. Do not add a second tier until one person has paid the first.

## 3. Find ten humans who already complained (20 minutes)

Ten real posts, threads, or emails. Copy the sentence they used. That is the language for the ask. Do not reply yet.

## 4. Write the ask (20 minutes)

Four lines, in their words: the complaint, the object, what they get this week, price + how to pay. Max 80 words. No "excited to share."

## 5. Send five (30 minutes)

Public reply or email they published. Not a DM blast. Then stop. If nobody answers in a week, change the sentence or the number. Do not change the stack.
